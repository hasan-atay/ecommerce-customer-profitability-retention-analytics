# Data Visualization — Power BI Report Design

The six PNGs in `screenshots/` are portfolio-ready visual previews of the intended Power BI report.
They are deliberately designed as management pages rather than chart collections.

## Page 01 — Executive Command Center
Purpose: answer whether growth is profitable and sustainable.
Core visuals: KPI strip, revenue/profit trend, executive signals.

## Page 02 — Commercial Performance
Purpose: explain revenue growth by customer type, category, channel and market.

## Page 03 — Customer Profitability
Purpose: separate high-revenue customers from high-profit customers.
Core visual: revenue vs profit scatter.

## Page 04 — Retention & Cohorts
Purpose: measure customer quality after acquisition.
Core visual: retention curve and cohort matrix.

## Page 05 — Discount & Returns
Purpose: quantify commercial leakage from promotions and returns.

## Page 06 — Management Actions
Purpose: turn analysis into an action queue with evidence and financial exposure.

The PNGs are visual references; the underlying Power BI report should implement the same layout,
interactions, slicers, drill-throughs and DAX-driven KPI behaviour.
