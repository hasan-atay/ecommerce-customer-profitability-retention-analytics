# Business Requirements

## Client scenario
An e-commerce business is growing but management is uncertain whether growth is profitable and sustainable.

## Required decisions
1. Understand revenue and profit trajectory.
2. Identify valuable customer segments.
3. Measure cohort retention and repeat purchasing.
4. Quantify discount economics.
5. Quantify return leakage.
6. Compare acquisition efficiency and long-term customer value.
7. Convert findings into prioritised management actions.

## Design principles
- Business question first.
- KPI definitions are controlled and documented.
- Star schema.
- Reusable DAX measures.
- Minimal visual clutter.
- Every page has a decision purpose.
- All data is synthetic.
