# KPI Dictionary

- Revenue: net sales after discounts.
- Gross Profit: revenue less COGS.
- Contribution Profit: revenue less COGS, shipping and payment costs.
- Gross Margin %: gross profit / revenue.
- Active Customers: distinct customers with qualifying purchases.
- New Customers: customers whose first qualifying purchase falls in the selected period.
- Repeat Purchase Rate: customers making a subsequent purchase / eligible acquired customers.
- AOV: revenue / completed orders.
- Discount Rate %: discount value / gross sales.
- Return Rate %: returned units or orders / corresponding sales denominator.
- Customer Profit: revenue less attributable operating, return and acquisition costs.
- CAC: acquisition spend / newly acquired customers.
- CLV: estimated lifetime contribution value under the documented retention assumptions.
